# Technical Stock Production drill: product selection

In Banmore Technical → Stock, Production drills for every stock area list only
individual products in the Product filter, without an `All` option. The first
available product alphabetically is selected by default. Actual, unit choices,
views and table rows follow that product. If no product is available, no product
is selected and no unclassified rows are presented as a selected product.

Building and Curing Production drills and other KPI drills keep their existing
product selection behavior. This does not change the Production KPI card or its
source queries.
